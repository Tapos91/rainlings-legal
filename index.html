Account and Data Deletion

You can request deletion of your Rainlings online profile and related data at any time from the dedicated deletion page.

Open Account and Data Deletion

Deleting online data can remove leaderboard entries, support history, online wallet/inventory recovery, and purchase recovery records connected to that profile.

Contact
